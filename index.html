How the numbers work

FP/G is projected fantasy points per game: the base categories times your weights, plus the expected bonus points per game.

Bonus points use the double-double and triple-double rates from your CSV when it has dd and td columns. Otherwise they are estimated from the averages with a normal model. Estimated rates are marked with an asterisk in the DD% column.

VORP/G is FP/G minus the replacement level at the player's best eligible position. Replacement level is the FP/G of the player ranked at (teams times starting slots at that position, with G, F and UTIL spread across positions, plus your bench depth setting).

Value is VORP/G times games played. It is the number the board ranks by.

Load player data

Paste per-game stats from a spreadsheet or CSV, or choose a file. The first row must be headers. Needed: name, pts, reb, ast. Optional: team, pos, gp, stl, blk, tov, 3pm, dd, td, adp.

Basketball Reference column names work as they are: Player, Tm, Pos, G, PTS, TRB, AST, STL, BLK, TOV, 3P. Repeated header rows and traded-player duplicates are skipped.

Use per-game averages, not season totals. dd and td are season totals when a gp column is present, and per-game rates (0 to 1) when it is not. adp is your draft ranking if you have one, lower is earlier.

Games played comes from the gp column. Without it each player gets 70.
